# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue about code, not about the person writing it. Assume
the other party is trying to build something good, and ask before you conclude
otherwise.

## Not welcome

Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks. Sexualised language or
imagery. Publishing anyone's private information. Sustained disruption of
discussion. Anything you would not say to a colleague standing next to you.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and anywhere else the
project is discussed, including when you are representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on
[this repository](https://github.com/hkyss/sonar/security) — it is the private
channel this project has. Reports stay between you and the maintainer.

Responses run from a private word, through editing or removing the content, to
blocking the account, depending on what happened and whether it kept happening.
